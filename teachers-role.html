Teachers’ Role in Inquiry Mathematics Instruction

Inquiry mathematics instruction depends on successful implementation by the teacher. These videos address instructional planning — using examples from the Structuring Numbers sequence to discuss how teachers select activities and tasks while considering students' reasoning — and demonstrate techniques for facilitating productive class discussions through a detailed analysis of a lesson excerpt.
