Coordinating Units

The Coordinating Units sequence helps children understand the base-10 place value system. It develops reasoning for coordinating groups of ten with individual units — foundational for addition and subtraction with numbers greater than twenty. The sequence has two parts: Part I focuses on single quantities, and Part II addresses sums and differences. Both parts move from physical materials (Unifix cubes) to reasoning through visual imagery, using an extended “candy shop” context (packing candies, circling candies, and candy flash activities).

We recommend watching the videos in order.
