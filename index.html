Inquiry Mathematics Resources

Free videos and notes to help teachers, teacher educators, professional developers, and math coaches build inquiry mathematics classrooms.

In an inquiry mathematics classroom, children figure out mathematical problems — often in more than one way — in ways that make sense to them, and they explain their methods by showing and explaining their ways to everyone in the classroom. The teacher initiates and guides the discussion by asking questions.

Creating this kind of classroom takes careful attention to both the mathematical content and the teacher's approach. The goal is in-depth conceptual development, rather than just acquisition of skills and procedures — because students are more successful with mathematics when it makes sense to them.
